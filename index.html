Magnetic axis off
The axis of the dipole that best fits Earth’s magnetic field, from the first three IGRF‑14 coefficients. Where it meets the surface are the geomagnetic poles — exactly opposite each other, and about 9° from the rotation axis.

These are not the magnetic dip poles, where a compass needle points straight down. Those come from the full field, do not sit opposite each other, and are the ones racing towards Siberia. In 2025 the geomagnetic north pole is at 80.8° N 72.8° W; the dip pole at 85.8° N 138.1° E.

Published tables quote geodetic latitude, roughly 0.06° higher than the geocentric value shown here.

Ocean floor on
The ocean in the standard texture is one flat colour — the Mariana Trench and the North Sea are literally the same blue. This swaps it for NASA's Blue Marble bathymetry, so ridges, trenches and shelves become visible. The land is unchanged. It replaces the day map rather than adding a layer, so it costs one download the first time you switch it on — about 0.4 MB at 2K, about 4.9 MB at 8K.
